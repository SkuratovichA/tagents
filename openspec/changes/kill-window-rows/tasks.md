## 1. Kill a window from its row

- [ ] 1.1 In `act()` of `lib/tagents/actions.sh`, send `kill` on a window key (`@*`) to `kill_agent "$pane" ""` and take `kill` out of the dimmed-row refusal, which keeps `beside`, `undock`, `send`, `rename` and `preview`; say so in the module's header comment. Proved by `tests/window-rows.sh`: `--act kill @<id>` with `y` piped in kills the window, and the R8 loop still sees `tagents: that window runs no chat` for `beside`, `undock`, `send` and `rename`.
- [ ] 1.2 `kill_agent` on a window key: a window that no longer exists shows `tagents: that window is gone` and asks nothing; a window that holds nothing but the sidebar shows `tagents: that is the dashboard itself` and kills nothing; any other window is asked about in the popup, its height grown by the pane lines the question prints, or inline where `prompt` declines. Proved by `tests/window-rows.sh`: the gone window gives the notice and no question, and the sidebar-only window and the sidebar survive with the notice shown.
- [ ] 1.3 `ask_kill` on a window key re-checks that the window exists and asks `kill this window?`: the window name, a dim `session:index` line, one dim line per pane except the sidebar with its index, its `#{pane_current_command}` and its `#{pane_current_path}` shortened by `tilde_of`, at most twelve of them and then `… and K more`, the line `the window closes with every pane in it`, and `the session ends with it` only when the window is the last of its session and the sidebar is not in it; then the `y confirms · anything else cancels` line and the bounded 60-second read that the agent question uses. Proved by `tests/window-rows.sh` on the printed question: every pane's command and directory, no line for the sidebar, the cap with its `… and 2 more` for fourteen panes, and the session line present only for a session's last window.
- [ ] 1.4 A new `end_window <window id>` closes a confirmed window: `leave_window_for_kill` first, then `tmux kill-window`, `signal_ev` and `resurrect_soon`, as `end_agent` does for a pane. Proved by `tests/window-rows.sh`: after `y` the window and all its panes are gone and every other window and the sidebar are where they were, and after `n`, an empty line or a closed stdin the window and its panes are still there.
- [ ] 1.5 `leave_window_for_kill` in `lib/tagents/rows.sh` takes a window id as well as a chat pane: for a window id the sidebar leaves whenever it is in that window, whatever else the window holds, to `landing_window` or to a new shell window of the same session. Proved by `tests/window-rows.sh`: killing the window the sidebar is in leaves the sidebar and the client in the landing window; killing the only window of a session with the sidebar in it leaves the session alive with a new window that holds the sidebar and is on screen; killing the only window of another session ends that session and leaves the client attached where it was.
